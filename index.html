The toolkit

Five tools, one operating picture

Each tool solves one real problem we met on site. Together they give management live, trustworthy numbers.

Flagship

Financial Intelligence System

A financial visibility platform for site-based operations with no ERP, HR system or accounting software to draw from. It replaces spreadsheet reporting with a real data system, fed entirely through simple web forms.

  • Site cash flow, expenses and revenue on demand
  • Simple web forms that anyone on site can use
  • No more rebuilding the month from a purchase report and a guess
Android app

BillScan

Photograph a purchase bill and AI reads it for you: printed or handwritten, English or Arabic, up to 3 pages. Every bill is checked, and the monthly purchase report builds itself.

  • Extracts date, bill no., rate, VAT, discount and total
  • Flags wrong totals, missing fields, duplicates and bills from another month
  • One tap exports the Excel report and a PDF of all bill photos
Automation

Smart Invoicing

Turns vendor bills into your branded tax invoices automatically, with markup, VAT and totals calculated for you.

  • Your letterhead and layout
  • Markup and VAT rules built in
  • Consistent file naming
SaaS

Attendance

Multi-company staff attendance using face recognition and geofencing: a mobile app for employees and a web portal for admins.

  • Face check-in on site only
  • Separate, secure data for each company
  • Admin dashboards and reports
Facilities

AssetPulse

Replaces paper building inspections ("health rounding") with a mobile app. Flags become coded action items that are tracked until they're closed.

  • Rounds by building and area
  • Coded action items with full history
  • Daily coverage dashboard

About

An engineer who got tired of spreadsheets

OpsNest was founded by an engineer with more than 20 years in facilities management, refinery and HVAC operations across the GCC and India.

Every tool began as a fix for a real bottleneck: bills retyped by hand, cash reconstructed at month end, attendance on paper, inspections lost in logbooks. OpsNest turns those fixes into products that other site-based businesses can use.
